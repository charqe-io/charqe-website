Company

What we do

Charqe is a software company based in Istanbul, Türkiye. We build our own products: mobile applications, web platforms and developer tools. When we take on a problem, we look at it not through one user’s eyes but through the eyes of everyone it affects.

  • 01 A single source of truth. We never store the same record twice; every party sees the same reality, limited to its role.
  • 02 Every surface speaks its own language. Showing the same data does not mean showing the same screen.
  • 03 Data responsibility comes first. Role-based access and data minimisation are built in from the start, not added later.
